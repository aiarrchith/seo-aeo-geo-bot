- Values quality, minimalism and certainty. Already shops Sephora. Refuses synthetics, fragrance and fillers.

## Voice and copy rules (hard rules for anything published)
- Never use em dashes.
- Don't name Ayurveda directly. Say plants and herbs instead. Only exception: the process name "18-Day Ayurvedic Phyto-Infusion".
- Never use "Ayurvedic-inspired". No Vata, Pitta, dosha or hormonal language anywhere.
- Say "botanicals", not "compounds".
- The product is a bakuchi seed oil. Never call it bakuchiol.
- Never call Reshma a "formulator".
- Never invent testimonials, reviews, ratings, studies or statistics.
- Never make medical claims. Skincare claims stay cosmetic ("helps skin look", "supports").
- Reshma's voice: like a voice note to a friend. Conversational, short punchy hooks, rhetorical questions, ends on empowerment, not selling.
- Journal/blog style reference: Fable & Mane's "Mane Monocle".
